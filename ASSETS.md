### Final generation prompt

Create a single original 3D brand illustration asset for letspets, an independent one-person digital creative studio that makes playful apps and games. This is brand artwork, NOT a product screenshot, NOT a website mockup. Use the attached reference only for its edge-framing composition and blue sky negative space, do NOT copy its people, masks, branding, text, or UI. Landscape 3:2 or 16:10, high resolution. A rich soft periwinkle blue sky (#829bbd around the center) with tactile playful abstract sculptures leaning in from the edges, as if seen from below inside a world of curiosity. Upper left: a huge soft mint green inflated looping arch with subtle rubber seams entering from the frame. Upper right: a glossy chrome curving tube catching blue reflections, a small butter-yellow ball near it. Lower left: warm apricot orange pleated/ribbed rubber spring partly cropped. Lower right: lilac inflated smooth sculptural star with rounded tips, partly cropped. Bottom middle: a tiny soft cloud far below, keep it understated. Sophisticated editorial 3D rendering, materials with subtle realistic texture, warm direct sunlight, ambient occlusion, gentle film grain, playful and premium rather than children toy advertising. Keep the central 46% width and top-to-bottom 65% area mostly open calm blue for white website typography overlay. Objects at edges large and boldly cropped, depth and floating feeling. No text, no letters, no wordmarks, no logo, no phone, no device, no interface, no people, no animals. Original artwork only.
